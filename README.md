### 👋 The short version

I didn't start in code. I spent 10 years in sales, field and service roles: managing a region of retail stores, running national field campaigns, selling B2B software, training teams. I learned how businesses really work, and where they leak time and money.

Then I started building the fixes myself. For the past 4 years I've designed and shipped CRMs, automations and AI agents for real businesses, and I run my own business on infrastructure I built and host myself.

That's my edge: **I understand the problem from the sales floor, and I can build the solution end to end, down to the server it runs on.**

### ⚙️ How I work

- **Start with the problem, not the tool.** Diagnose how the business really runs before building anything.
- **Design the data first:** schemas, ERDs and how everything connects, built from scratch.
- **Ship the MVP first,** then iterate with the people who use it.
- **Run it in production:** self-hosted, monitored, secured, and fixed when it breaks.
- **Adoption is part of the job:** documentation and training until it runs without me.
